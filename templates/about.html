⚖️ Lexpert

Your Digital Legal Companion

Lexpert is your intelligent legal companion — built to help citizens instantly understand the IPC section, punishment, and fine behind any offense in India. It simplifies complex legal information into a user-friendly platform.

🔍 Smart Search

Type or select any offense to instantly get IPC details, fine, and punishment info.

📞 Victim Helpdesk

Provides official helpline numbers and legal assistance links based on selected offense.

📂 Full IPC Directory

Explore over 200 categorized legal offenses with clear and concise explanations.

🌗 Light/Dark Mode

Switch easily between dark and light themes for better reading and visibility.

🛠️ Technologies Used

  • Backend: Python (Flask)
  • Frontend: HTML5 + CSS3
  • Database: JSON (200+ entries)
  • UX: Desktop-friendly with Light/Dark theme

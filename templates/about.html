⚖️ Lexpert

Your Digital Legal Companion

Lexpert is your intelligent legal companion — built to help citizens instantly understand the IPC section, punishment, and fine behind each offense in India. It simplifies complex legal information into a user-friendly, accessible platform.

🔍 Smart Search

Type or select any offense to get instant info on IPC sections, punishments, and fines.

📞 Victim Helpdesk

Provides official helpline numbers and legal assistance links for immediate support.

📂 Extensive Directory

Explore 200+ categorized offenses with clear explanations, legal sections, and penalties.

🌗 Seamless Themes

Toggle easily between light and dark modes for comfortable reading anytime.

  • SQLite
  • Jinja2 Templating
  • HTML5 & CSS3
  • JavaScript (for dynamic UI)
  • Werkzeug Security
